03 / FAQ

The important bits.

Twing Twing is an informational monitoring service, not investment advice or trade execution. Alerts may be delayed or missed because of blockchain, indexing, network, Telegram, or telephony failures. Never share a seed phrase or private key.

What counts as one alert?

One alert means one active rule. That rule can trigger repeatedly while it remains enabled.

Do you need access to my wallet?

No. Twing Twing monitors public on-chain activity and should never need your private key or seed phrase.

Can I combine multiple wallets?

Yes. Professional rules can trigger when several tracked wallets meet the same condition within a chosen time window.

Are calls guaranteed to arrive?

No. Delivery depends on third-party infrastructure, so Twing Twing should not be your only source of critical trading information.
